Does floor polishing cause dust or damage home furniture? expand_more
No. Our process uses 100% water-cooled wet diamond grinding discs, capturing all debris as slurry without airborne dust. We wrap baseboards and walls with protective film and vacuum residue immediately, making it completely safe for fully furnished homes and villas in Hafar Al-Batin.
What is the difference between regular floor waxing and Italian diamond crystallization? expand_more
Wax only creates a temporary superficial film that yellows, traps dirt, and scratches quickly. Italian diamond crystallization mechanically micro-planes the stone surface and chemically seals calcium crystals, creating a scratch-resistant, mirror-like gloss that lasts for years and resists stains naturally.
