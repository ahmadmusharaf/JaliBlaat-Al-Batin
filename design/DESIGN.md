## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-craft, authoritative brand identity tailored specifically for specialized floor restoration, terrazzo grinding, crystalline surface treatment, and Italian marble polishing throughout Hafar Al-Batin and the surrounding Eastern Province. 

### Brand Character & Aesthetic
The visual direction fuses **Architectural Prestige** with **Haptic Cleanliness**. It departs decisively from generic maintenance websites by honoring the physical characteristics of polished natural stone: geometric precision, liquid mirror reflections, cool mineral weights, and immaculate chamfered edges.

- **Primary Persona:** Elite residential villa owners, commercial facility managers, and luxury interior architects in Hafar Al-Batin demanding flawless diamond crystallization rather than quick chemical washes.
- **Emotional Impact:** Absolute confidence, mineral perfection, quiet luxury, and local mastery. Interfaces feel as solid and meticulously finished as honed Thassos or Royal Cream marble.
- **Design Movement:** Modern Architectural Minimalism elevated with Neoclassical Gulf luxury details—featuring razor-sharp typographic scale, soft champagne hairline borders, high-clarity surface contrasts, and fluid bilingual symmetry.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the environment of pristine stone craftsmanship under refined interior lighting. Contrast ratios rigorously comply with WCAG AAA for editorial content and WCAG AA for dynamic interactive elements.

### Color Archetypes
- **Imperial Navy (`#0B2545`):** The primary brand anchor. Used for dominant headings, structural footers, primary navigation cards, and hero sections to evoke timeless structural permanence.
- **Deep Slate / Mineral Ink (`#17212B`):** The primary reading ink. Delivers softer, warmer text clarity than harsh absolute black across long-form Arabic specifications and service manifests.
- **Champagne Gold (`#C8A96A`):** The badge of artisanal mastery. Applied to micro-borders, diamond disc iconography, seal certification emblems, and subtle framing accents to mimic metallic inlay in terrazzo.
- **Warm Ivory (`#FAF7F0`):** The primary canvas background. Replaces sterile digital white with the organic warmth of untreated Crema Marfil limestone.
- **Polished Off-White (`#EFEBE4`):** Secondary container surface. Provides alternating rhythm for technical comparison tables, quote calculation zones, and neighborhood grids.
- **Direct WhatsApp Green (`#25D366`):** Reserved strictly for instant conversion hooks, click-to-chat triggers, and urgent estimate dispatching. Paired with pure white or deep navy text to ensure uncompromised legibility.

## Typography

The typographical architecture solves the complex challenge of authentic bilingual balance in a high-ticket craft niche. Arabic serves as the primary visual foundation (`dir="rtl"`), supported seamlessly by Latin elements (`dir="ltr"`).

### Typographic Rules & Line Rhythms
- **Arabic Text (IBM Plex Sans Arabic):** Classical Nastaliq and standard modern geometric typefaces either crowd horizontal space or clip vocalization marks. IBM Plex Sans Arabic provides balanced baseline discipline, open counters, and high architectural harmony. Arabic body text must maintain an expanded line-height multiplier between `1.75` and `1.85` to avoid diacritic collision and guarantee effortless reading for older property owners.
- **Latin & Numerical Text (Inter):** Metric figures, technical square-meter measurements ($m^2$), machine RPM indicators, phone numbers, and English parallel headers utilize Inter. Its tabular numeral figures align cleanly against Arabic text without baseline sinking.
- **Optical Kerning:** English capital labels and micro-tags utilize slight letter-spacing (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`). Arabic typography must never use artificial tracking or character-spacing, relying exclusively on true typographic weights and organic kashida-free typesetting.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built upon a 12-column responsive grid engineered to showcase grand spatial transformation. It accommodates bidirectional layout flipping (`RTL` to `LTR`) without visual compromise.

### Layout System
- **Desktop Grid:** 12-column fluid grid locked to a max-width container of `1280px`. Gutters are fixed at `1.5rem` (`24px`), with outer margins stepping from `3rem` (`48px`) to `4rem` (`64px`) on ultra-wide viewports.
- **Tablet Grid (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) canvas margins.
- **Mobile Grid (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and `1.25rem` (`20px`) safe margin gutters.
- **RTL Fluidity:** Spatial flows mirror symmetrically. Left-anchored chevrons, trailing telephone icons, and directional sliders invert positions automatically using logical CSS properties (`padding-inline`, `margin-inline`, `inset-inline-start`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth reflects the tactile polish of marble: flat surfaces with razor-fine hairline highlights, calibrated specular diffusion, and layered tonal containment. Thick, muddy drop-shadows are strictly prohibited.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#FAF7F0` or `#EFEBE4` without shadow. Used for page-level backgrounds and broad structural bands.
2. **Level 1 (Subtle Chamfer - Cards & Modules):** Grounded panels feature a `1px` continuous border in `#C8A96A` at `20%` opacity (`rgba(200, 169, 106, 0.20)`), overlaid onto pure `#FFFFFF` or `#0B2545`. Casts an ultra-diffused floor-contact shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.05)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Polished Lift - Interactive States & Highlights):** Interactive hover states and featured pricing modules elevate cleanly using dual-stage ambient light: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08)`, accented with a sharper champagne border highlight (`rgba(200, 169, 106, 0.45)`).
4. **Level 3 (Overlay & Conversion Anchors):** Floating top navigation and sticky bottom conversion bars use backdrop-blur glassmorphism: `background: rgba(250, 247, 240, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom or top hairline stroke of `rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary is governed by **Soft Precision** (`roundedness: 1`). It mirrors the micro-beveled edging applied by diamond stones to freshly cut marble slabs to prevent chipping while maintaining architectural crispness.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to badges, inputs, technical tags, and neighborhood district chips.
- **Container Radius (0.5rem / 8px):** Applied to service cards, before-and-after comparison viewports, modals, and image frames.
- **Accent Elements:** Conversion action buttons and quick-dial buttons employ a slightly more generous `0.5rem` radius or pill shapes specifically for emergency floating triggers, establishing clear tactile affordance.

## Components

### Header & Sticky Topbar
- **Structure:** Dual-tier utility bar. Upper micro-tier displays Hafar Al-Batin service status, business hours, and language switcher (العربية / English). Lower tier carries the bilingual wordmark, core service links, and quick estimate trigger.
- **Styling:** Grounded in `#0B2545` with crisp Champagne Gold micro-dividers. Transitions into a blurred sticky header upon scroll.

### Conversion Anchors (WhatsApp & Direct Call)
- **WhatsApp Action:** Styled with `#25D366` background, paired with high-contrast `#FFFFFF` typography and an official WhatsApp icon. Pulsing ambient glow indicates active dispatchers.
- **Direct Phone Action:** Bordered in solid `#C8A96A` with `#0B2545` fill or crisp inverted ivory, featuring localized click-to-call direct dialing with Eastern Province availability messaging.
- **Sticky Bottom Mobile Bar:** Docked to the mobile viewport base. Features a dual-split layout: 60% width dedicated to one-tap WhatsApp instant quote dispatch; 40% dedicated to direct call.

### Before & After Interactive Surface Showcase
- **Mechanism:** Split-screen slider with a tactile central drag handle rendered as a diamond-cut champagne disc.
- **Visuals:** High-resolution uncompressed imagery comparing dull, scratched, or stained stone (limestone, terrazzo, crystal marble) directly against the mirror-finish crystalline restoration.
- **Overlays:** Micro-labels on opposite corners (`قبل الترميم` / `Before` and `بعد الجلي والكريستال` / `After Polish`) with frosted dark glass backing.

### Service Cards with Diamond Disc Motifs
- **Anatomy:** Structured card with a top-aligned technical vector icon representing diamond grinding heads, Italian planetary polishers, or crystallizing compounds.
- **Framing:** Base `#FFFFFF` with a `1px` champagne border (`rgba(200, 169, 106, 0.25)`). Hover state shifts the card upward by `4px`, deepening the ambient gold rim.
- **Content:** Includes Arabic service title, brief technical scope (e.g., تسوية الفواصل، معالجة الرخام بالجولي، التلميع بالماس والكريستال), duration indicator, and an explicit quote inquiry link.

### District Service Coverage Badges
- **Purpose:** Local authority chips confirming rapid mobilization across Hafar Al-Batin neighborhoods.
- **Target Districts:** العزيزية (Al Aziziyah), الخالدية (Al Khalidiyah), المحمدية (Al Mohammadiyah), البلدية (Al Baladiyah), أبو موسى الأشعري (Abu Musa Al Ashari), الروضة (Al Rawdah), and surrounding zones.
- **Styling:** Compact `#EFEBE4` pill containers with `#0B2545` text, prefaced by a subtle gold location pin icon. Active/Selected states invert to `#0B2545` fill with `#C8A96A` text.

### Structured FAQ Accordion
- **Design:** Deep Slate titles on Warm Ivory panels separated by delicate hairline rules. 
- **States:** Collapsed items display an elegant gold plus (`+`) glyph; expanded items reveal comprehensive answers regarding diamond disc grits, curing times, dustless grinding systems, and crystalline sealer longevity, toggling to a minus (`−`) glyph.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Dispatcher
- **Fields:** Clean architectural inputs framed with `1px` `#EFEBE4` borders, transitioning to `#C8A96A` focus rings. Native right-to-left placeholder text alignment with clear bilingual units (e.g., المساحة بالمتر المربع / Area in $m^2$).
